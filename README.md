# LeadRescuePro private ops

Private business operating repository for LeadRescuePro.

## Current business rules

- Target market: U.S. plumbing companies only for now.
- Offer: separate AI receptionist number for inbound plumbing calls.
- The AI receptionist qualifies ZIP/service area, captures issue, urgency, and callback details, then logs leads in a dashboard.
- Pricing: $997 setup, $499/month base, $0.50 per billable minute.
- Month-to-month.
- No revenue guarantee.
- No fake case studies or testimonials.
- Public community identity: `CallOps Help` for forums/Reddit and `LeadRescuePro Community` for professional platforms.
